# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Recruit heroes and send them gathering, questing, into dungeons and weekly raids.
- Crafting, a living market, buildings, mounts, pets, achievements and New Era prestige.
- Bags tab laid out like the game's own bags: every material, consumable and spare piece of gear as a slot.
- Quests refill: a new quest takes the place of each one you start.
- Offline progress: repeating tasks keep going for up to 24 hours while you are logged out.
- Options page (Options > AddOns > Warcamp): minimap button, window scale, export / import save,
  backup restore, reset.
- Saves are protected across updates: a backup is kept before any save change, and data an update
  removes is cleaned out instead of breaking the camp.
